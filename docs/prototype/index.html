A familiar shorthand, a little help

First line: 03.10 any workout title (or 03.10.2026). Titles are free text, not a fixed list. Optional intake: (680/37).

One exercise per line: жим в нахилі сміт 60-10 -9 50-11 -10
60-10 = 60 kg × 10 reps. -9 repeats the last weight. 20- is a planned set. You can leave any unfinished text in your draft.
